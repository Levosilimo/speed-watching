# Russian-language corpus — provenance

A 16-video Russian corpus used for the per-language rate model (language
validation gates G1/G2 in docs/phase0-caption-wpm.md). The captions are the
work of their creators; copyright remains with them.

`ru-corpus-summary.json` (the per-record classification and gate results) is
committed. `ru-corpus.jsonl` holds the full verbatim transcript lines: it is
regenerated on demand by the harness and is not committed.
