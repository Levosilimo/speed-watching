# Web-layer re-run captures — provenance

Raw `/api/timedtext` responses (pb3 wire format, ASR track re-picked) captured
by the web-rerun pass of the corpus harness (scripts/measure-corpus.ts) for
the videos that failed first-pass capture. The captions are the work of their
creators; copyright remains with them.

The `web-*.json3` transcripts are regenerated on demand by the harness and are
not committed; `rerun-results.jsonl` (the per-video analysis rows) is
committed.
